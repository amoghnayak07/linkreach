# Style file

linkreach writes every email draft and connection note to match this file. Keep the three
headings below; edit everything under them. Your real copy is `style.md` next to
`config.yaml` and is never committed.

## Instructions

- Subject: the role title, then my name, e.g. "Product Manager II - Naveen Murali". No clickbait, under 60 characters.
- Open with "Hi <first name>," on its own line.
- First paragraph: one specific, true observation about their work or team, tied to the role.
- Second paragraph: the one or two background points that map most directly to the role. Use real numbers from my background only.
- Third paragraph: a single, easy ask (a 15-minute chat, or who the right person is), then the resume link.
- Sign off with "Best," and my first name.
- Plain, warm, direct. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no flattery, no buzzwords.
- Aim for 120 to 160 words in the body.
- Connection note: one or two sentences, mention the role, no link.

## Example emails

Subject: Product Manager II - Naveen Murali

Hi Priya,

Your team's write-up on moving Azure onboarding from three screens to one was the clearest case for fewer steps I've read this year, and it's the kind of work the Product Manager II role on your team describes.

I've spent three years shipping AI features into enterprise SaaS. At Bizom I owned an AI-enabled ERP platform across 15+ clients and shipped an image-recognition module that lifted conversion by 25%.

Would you have 15 minutes in the next couple of weeks to tell me what the team needs most from this hire? My resume: https://example.com/naveen-murali-resume.pdf

Best,
Naveen
